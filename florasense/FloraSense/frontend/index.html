About FloraSense IoT

FloraSense is an advanced smart-agriculture IoT platform integrating real physical ESP32 microcontrollers, DHT11 sensors, analog soil moisture probes, digital LDRs, and MongoDB Atlas for real-time plant care automation.

Hardware Pinout (ESP32 Dev Module):
  • DHT11 Climate Data: GPIO 5
  • Soil Moisture Sensor (AO): GPIO 34 (12-bit ADC)
  • LDR Light Sensor (DO): GPIO 27
  • Green LED (Optimal): GPIO 23
  • Orange LED (Alert): GPIO 19
  • Red LED (Danger): GPIO 26
  • LCD 16x2 I2C: SDA GPIO 21, SCL GPIO 22 (0x27)
